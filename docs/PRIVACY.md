# PropCheck privacy policy

_Last updated: 6 November 2026_

PropCheck is an offline calculator for the cost of buying a flat. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter.

## What we collect
Nothing. PropCheck has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and it makes no network requests.

## What stays on your phone
Everything you type (prices, areas, income, loan details, locality, checklist ticks and notes) is stored only in a database on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## When data leaves your phone
Only when you choose to:
- **Export backup** creates a JSON file and opens your phone's share sheet. Where it goes next (email, cloud drive, another app) is up to you.
- **Links** to MahaRERA, IGR Maharashtra, news articles and other sources open in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

## Imports
Risk data and backups you import are read from files you pick or text you paste. They are stored on your device only.

## Children
PropCheck is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will ship with an app update and be posted at this address.
