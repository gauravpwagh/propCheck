## Full description
The quoted price is never what you pay. PropCheck adds up everything else so you can plan properly before you book.

**See the full cost**
- Stamp duty (on the higher of agreement value and ready reckoner value), registration, GST for under-construction flats, including the 1% affordable-housing slab
- Parking and other builder extras, corpus, advance maintenance, society transfer fee
- Brokerage, renovation for older buildings, lawyer fees, interiors
- Loan processing fee, stamp duty on the loan, pre-EMI interest and rent while you wait for possession

**Work backwards from your budget**
Enter what you can spend in total and PropCheck finds the highest quoted price you can afford.

**Check your probable home loan**
Estimates the loan a bank is likely to offer from your income, existing EMIs, age, the property value and the building's age, and tells you which limit applies.

**Compare flats side by side**
Save properties and compare total cost, cash needed, EMI, ₹ per sq ft (carpet and all-in) and red flags.

**Buy safely**
A step-by-step checklist for under-construction, ready and resale flats, warnings such as a missing RERA number or OC, and Mumbai scam patterns with recent reported cases and official links to verify.

**Private by design**
Works completely offline. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your phone.

v1 covers 1RK, 1BHK and 2BHK flats in Mumbai (BMC). Rates as of November 2026; you can edit them in Settings.

PropCheck gives estimates only. It is not legal or financial advice.
